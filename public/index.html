Fixing one

  1. Take one item. Read its issue and reproduce it on the live page at 390 px.
  2. Say on the issue that you are taking it.
  3. Fix it in the repo that owns the page: the smallest change, as a pull request linked to the issue.
  4. Post the before and after on the issue. It stays open until the fix is live and checked.
